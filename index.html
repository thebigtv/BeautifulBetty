Our Philosophy

An urban retreat
in the heart
of Hexham

Founded by Hayley Stokoe in 2015, Beautiful Betty is Hexham's first ethical, sustainable and conscious beauty salon and beauty shop — housed in a beautiful 3-storey listed building just steps from the town centre.

We believe beauty should never cost the earth. Every product we stock, every treatment we offer, and every decision we make is guided by our commitment to conscious beauty — cruelty-free, eco-friendly, and proudly independent.

Step inside and discover neutral tones, leafy green plants, natural colours and wood finishes — a space designed for calm, tranquillity, and genuine human connection. Our team of highly motivated, professional women are here for you.
